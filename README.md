# Brief Background
## Smart grids generate continuous load and grid health data.
## Accurate forecasting supports demand and asset monitoring.

# Existing Limitations
## Electricity patterns change over time.
## AutoCon uses one global autocorrelation pattern
## Fixed correlation may miss recent demand shifts.
## Autocorrelation mainly captures linear relationships.

# Our Solution 
## We propose an interactive analytics platform for adaptive electricity-demand and transformer-condition forecasting.
## The system combines recent and long-term temporal patterns, supports scenario-based simulation, and provides forecasts, risk alerts, and decision support for energy planning and predictive maintenance.

# Target Users 
## Grid operators , Electricity distributors , Maintenance teams , Energy planners
